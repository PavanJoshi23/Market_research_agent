## Executive Summary

TempleConnect is a web-first, pan-India platform to help devotees discover temples (history, timings, photos, audio/video) and to enable temples to sell and manage poojas, sevas and related services online. The refined market view shows a $207B global religious tourism market (2026) with India representing a large, fast-growing religious & spiritual market (USD ~70B SAM). A practical early target is temples as customers (pan-India rollout after a single-city pilot). The product differentiates by pursuing an "official-first" partnership model with temple trusts, combining curated heritage content and discovery with an integrated temple operations toolkit (real-time sloting, priest scheduling, payments/payouts, livestream and prasad fulfilment). Monetization mixes transaction fees, subscriptions for temple SaaS, and revenue-share logistics. Technical and GTM choices emphasize speed-to-market and trust: a modular-monolith stack (Next.js + Supabase), Razorpay/Stripe payments, Twilio/WhatsApp + SendGrid notifications, and Google Maps for discovery. Launch strategy: a 6-month pilot in one metro (onboard 10–30 temples, build 50–200 SEO-optimized temple pages), then expand regionally to achieve ~USD 350M SOM (estimated ~0.5% of India SAM) over 3–5 years with strong focus on legal MoUs, privacy-by-design, fraud controls and festival-scale capacity planning.

## Technical & Marketing Strategy

### Technical Strategy

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Recommended Stack | Next.js (React + TypeScript) — SEO-friendly server-rendered website and admin dashboards - Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Realtime) — backend, auth and realtime booking locks - Razorpay (India) and Stripe (global) — payments, refunds and payouts - Google Maps / Places API (fallback Mapbox/Geoapify) — discovery, geocoding and POI data - Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp) + SendGrid/Postmark — notifications, OTPs and transactional email |
| Architecture | Start as a modular monolith: Next.js frontend + Supabase Postgres backend for fastest MVP delivery. Push heavy/async jobs (webhooks, notifications, PDF receipts, streaming ingestion) to serverless Edge/Functions. Use Supabase Realtime and row-level booking locks to prevent double-booking; evolve into microservices (booking, payments, search) only when justified by scale. |
| Key Integrations | Google Maps / Places API for temple discovery and geolocation (Mapbox/Geoapify fallback) - Razorpay (India) / Stripe (global) for payments, payouts and subscription billing - Twilio or WhatsApp Business API for booking confirmations, OTPs and festival alerts - SendGrid or Postmark for transactional email and receipts - Streaming provider / CDN for live darshan (optional) and logistics partners/APIs for prasad fulfilment & tracking |
| Build Approach | Buy managed building blocks (Supabase, Razorpay/Stripe, Google Maps, Twilio, SendGrid) to reduce ops. Build in-house the differentiated domain logic: availability/booking engine (capacity-aware, predictive queueing), pooja/catalog productization, temple/provider onboarding flows, reconciliation/settlement tooling and verified trust primitives (badges, receipts, ritual proof). Validate via a metro pilot, then iterate. |

### How Your Product Should Be Unique

- Official-first discovery & trust model: verified partnerships with temple trusts (official badges, live status and direct embeds) so listings are authoritative rather than community-curated.
- Integrated end-to-end ritual productization: discovery + booking + livestream + prasad fulfilment and delivery tracking (photos/video/digital certificate) in one transaction.
- Heritage-led, multilingual content layer: scholarship-grade temple stories, audio/video narrations and festival timelines to drive organic traffic and deeper engagement.
- Capacity-aware bookings & predictive queueing: real-time sloting, predictive wait times, smart recommendations for quieter windows and family/group booking flows.
- Temple management toolkit & fair revenue-share: dashboard for slot management, pandit assignment, livestream management and automated reconciliation to convert temples into active partners.
