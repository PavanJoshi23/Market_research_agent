# Research Report: I want to build a online platform that allows users to discover temples and explore their history, timings etc.. also enable devotees to book pooja and manage serivces online.

> Generated by I2M Research Agent · 2026-02-25 15:21

## Executive Summary

There is a sizable and growing faith-tech opportunity (TAM ~$4B) for a platform that combines temple discovery, verified historical content and secure online pooja/seva booking. Competition today is fragmented between back-office temple ERPs, white-label app builders and early devotional marketplaces, leaving room for a vertically integrated consumer marketplace that also delivers operational value to temples. Key risks are regulatory/social sensitivity, low temple adoption, booking/scale reliability and payment/compliance exposures. Recommended strategy is a region-focused pilot (10–25 temples) that proves increased donations/bookings through verified temple partnerships, SEO-led discovery, diaspora-oriented payments and live-darshan features, while using managed infrastructure and API partnerships with existing temple ERPs to reduce build time. The product’s unique position will be built on scholar/archival-vetted temple pages with provenance badges, temple-verified booking workflows, a vetted priest/seva marketplace and a low-cost onboarding model for small temples — delivering a trust-first, diaspora-ready temple discovery + transaction platform.

## Technical & Marketing Strategy

### Technical Strategy

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Recommended Stack | Next.js (React) for web frontend (SSG/ISR for SEO) · Supabase (Postgres + Auth + realtime) or Firebase for backend/data + auth · Vercel for hosting + serverless functions (or AWS Lambda with API Gateway if prefer AWS) · Mux or Agora for live/recorded video streaming; Stripe + Razorpay for payments · Sanity or Strapi headless CMS for temple content (history, photos, articles) |
| Architecture | Serverless modular architecture: frontend as Next.js app with SSG/ISR pages for each temple (good for SEO), serverless API functions for bookings/payments/notifications, a managed Postgres DB for transactional data, a headless CMS for editorial content, and separate managed services for streaming and payments. Keep domain services (booking engine, temple admin panel, content) as bounded modules (not full microservices at launch) to reduce complexity. |
| Key Integrations | Payments: Razorpay (India) + Stripe (global) for bookings/donations · Streaming: Mux (VOD + live) or Agora for live darshan/recorded pooja videos · Maps & Discovery: Google Maps Platform or Mapbox for temple search, directions and geolocation · Comm: Twilio/WhatsApp Business API and SendGrid for SMS/email/booking confirmations |
| Build Approach | Mix of buy + build. Buy managed services for payments, streaming, maps, and comms. Use an off-the-shelf headless CMS (Sanity/Strapi) and Supabase/Firebase to accelerate backend. Build custom booking workflow, temple discovery UX, availability/slot engine, and the temple admin dashboard (integration points with existing Temple ERPs like TempleStack/3ioSetu should be optional via APIs). Consider partnerships/integrations with existing Temple ERP providers rather than re-building full accounting/donation compliance modules. |

Keep CAC low by leaning on partnerships, referrals, and content-driven organic traffic.

### How Your Product Should Be Unique

- Temple discovery + verified heritage content: combine a searchable geo-index of temples with scholar/archival-curated pages (history, architecture, festival calendar, audio guides and vetted photos) and a 'verified' provenance badge so users can discover contextual stories, not just contact info.
- Transactional trust & live experience: integrate temple-verified live schedules, slot-level booking that ties to an authenticated temple admin workflow, instant SMS/WhatsApp confirmations, live-darshan streaming or priest video-call options, plus trackable digital prasad delivery (courier integration) and automated GST/donation receipts to eliminate fraud concerns.
- Built-for-small-temples commercial model & rapid onboarding: offer a free or low-cost core tier for community/small temples with a simple WhatsApp/USSD onboarding flow and QR-based on-site sync (so offline temples can accept online bookings); monetize via optional feature add-ons and a capped commission (transparent fee cap) rather than high monthly SaaS.
- Priest & seva marketplace with governance features: provide a vetted priest/pujarī marketplace (background checks, identity verification, ratings), earmarked donation options, accounting export for trusts, and a volunteer coordination module so temples get operational value beyond bookings.
- Diaspora & accessibility-first UX: timezone-aware scheduling and multi-currency international payments, multi-language audio/text guides, assisted booking via call/WhatsApp for elderly users, and a pilgrimage planner that bundles temple timings, route maps and optional travel/stay partners for multi-temple trips.

> **Positioning Statement:** For devotees (domestic and international) who want authentic temple discovery plus secure online pooja booking and live participation, TempleConnect is an online temple discovery & booking platform that combines curator-vetted historical content with temple-verified booking, live darshan and logistics; unlike TempleStack or generic faith‑tech marketplaces, our product couples scholarly-curated temple pages and provenance badges with a low-cost onboarding model, verified priest marketplace and diaspora-first payments and delivery features.
